# Report design contract

This report follows `../performance-work-2026-10-14/artifact.md`. The formatting authority is `../WaterFlex_Scheduler_Algorithm_Comparison_Explained.docx` (SHA-256 `5366f3017d6f18c15de295ba5fb9c1a014b6222911040f1c5e044845e57f832d`). Its styles.xml, stylesWithEffects.xml, numbering.xml, theme, fontTable.xml and footer parts are kept byte for byte, and the body is replaced.

**Page and type:**

- one portrait Letter section
- Calibri
- black headings
- pale blue DCE6EF table headers
- alternating FFFFFF and F4F6F8 rows

**Tables:**

- Header rows repeat, and no table row splits.
- Tables of twelve or fewer rows stay on one page.
- Appendix and wide tables use 9 pt cell text; body tables use 10 pt.

**Narrative:** each table is explained by a figure next to it. The report runs in this order:

1. scope
2. the mechanism (why the fairness phase was slow)
3. the bars
4. what the extra moves buy
5. the policy comparison
6. the next profile leaders
7. conclusions
8. recommendations
9. complete appendices

**Figures:**

- Each arm keeps one color across all eleven charts.
- The two diagrams use the diagram-design default skin, with burnt-tangerine as the single accent on the promoted path.
- Captions say how to read the figure, not only what it shows.

**Wording:**

- Every speed is a median of paired ratios.
- Every variance is in thousandths.
- Every cost is in cents unless it says dollars.
- Lower is fairer on every measure.
- Interpretation beyond the recorded fields is labeled as such, for example which caller MapN.probe stands for.

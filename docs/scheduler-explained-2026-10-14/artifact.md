# Report design contract

Follows `../daily-tabu-variants-5d15129b/artifact.md`. The formatting authority is `../WaterFlex_Scheduler_Algorithm_Comparison_Explained.docx` (SHA-256 `5366f3017d6f18c15de295ba5fb9c1a014b6222911040f1c5e044845e57f832d`). Its styles.xml, stylesWithEffects.xml, numbering.xml, theme, fontTable.xml and footer parts are preserved byte for byte; the body is replaced.

**Page and type:** one portrait Letter section, Calibri, black headings, pale blue DCE6EF table headers, and alternating FFFFFF and F4F6F8 rows.

**Tables:**
- Header rows repeat, and no table row splits.
- Tables of twelve or fewer rows stay on one page.
- Tables appear only in the appendices: the studies index and the definitions and measures. The body uses figures and prose instead.
- Appendix tables use 9 pt cell text.
- Long tables keep their first two and last three rows together, so no row is stranded under a repeated header.

**Pagination:** each heading, the paragraphs under it and the figure they introduce are chained with keep with next whenever the block's estimated height (text length, the figure's aspect ratio at 6.85 inches, and its caption) fits one page; a block that would not fit is left to break, and the build prints any such block. Spacing is never made with blank paragraphs.

**Narrative:** five parts: the stateless API, opening with the scheduler as a service of its own and then explained figure by figure from ownership to routing (Part I); how the work was measured (Part II); the five optimization threads in the order the evidence arrived, each told as first iteration, measurement, change, evidence and decision and closing with what it handed on (Part III); the system as it stands (Part IV); and what comes next, performance and features (Part V). The prose names no date on which a feature shipped or a decision was made; study dates appear only in the Appendix A index. Appendix A indexes the studies and Appendix B defines terms and measures; there is no reference appendix and no numbered citation. The portal is not described. Every quantitative claim is computed from a validated source at build time; the two claims that are quoted from reports rather than recomputed (the field validation's fleet split of K opt, the budget study) say so in the text and in EVIDENCE.md.

**Figures:** all 55 follow the diagram-design skill's grammar (architecture, sequence, flowchart, state machine, layer stack, data model, heatmap, bar, diverging bar, line, dumbbell, stacked bar and scatter types) on a 980-wide canvas, so a 12 px label prints at about 6 pt at the 6.85 inch page width (1.31 times the earlier 1280 canvas); each figure was relaid for the narrower canvas rather than scaled, and checked with the skill's self check and geometry verifier. In the document every figure is placed at the same 6.85 inch width, so every label prints at the same size. The skin is white paper, ink #1F2A33, steel blue #7DAFCB service nodes, grey #C9C9C9 stores, and one green accent #287A4C reserved for the focal element of each figure (the choice made, the build that shipped, the headline reply). Comparisons are greys and muted blues so the accent stays the only signal. Every bar axis starts at zero, signed charts draw their zero line, the routing chart is a labelled log scale, and ticks are round; every legend is a strip under the plot.

**The later edition (2026-10-22):** updated in place, keeping the folder and file name so links hold. The cover carries only the edition date, October 22 2026 (owner's edit in Word, carried into the generator along with the removal of the management port note and of the closing lines of Parts II and IV). The work after October 14 is woven into the thread it extends, under subheads that begin "Since the first edition", rather than told as a sixth thread: the third daily choice and the stall stop in section 9, the refinement cap, pool layout and dynamic horizon in section 10, and the engine's arithmetic and its verification on the stack in section 13. Rejected treatments (S, formula c, CR11) are shown in grey beside what shipped, and W's shipping under its bar is named as the owner's decision. Study K (#164) stays out.

**Wording:** every latency is the caller's, waits included, and the text says so. Comparisons are between fresh-seed runs only; the two halves of the load series are never read as one curve; savings from different solver studies are never pooled, each study's own control is its comparison. Multithreaded solving is described as planned work with its gate and its cost, with no edition classification. No em or en dash anywhere.

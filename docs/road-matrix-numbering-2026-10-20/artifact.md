# Report design contract

This report follows `../fairness-measures-2026-10-20/artifact.md`, which follows `../performance-work-2026-10-14/artifact.md`. The formatting authority is `../WaterFlex_Scheduler_Algorithm_Comparison_Explained.docx` (SHA-256 `5366f3017d6f18c15de295ba5fb9c1a014b6222911040f1c5e044845e57f832d`).
Its styles.xml, stylesWithEffects.xml, numbering.xml, theme, fontTable.xml and footer parts are kept byte for byte, and the body is replaced.

**Page and type:**

- one portrait Letter section;
- Calibri;
- black headings;
- pale blue DCE6EF table headers;
- alternating FFFFFF and F4F6F8 rows.

**Tables:**

- Header rows repeat, and no table row splits.
- Tables of twelve or fewer rows stay on one page.
- Appendix and wide tables use 9 pt cell text; body tables use 10 pt.

**Narrative:** each table is explained by a figure next to it. The report runs in this order:

1. scope (the two trees and six arms);
2. the mechanism (what the profile found, and how the fix removes it);
3. the fix's speed, by phase and fleet;
4. what the extra moves bought;
5. b and c against the bars on the faster engine;
6. load balance measured again;
7. the next profile leaders;
8. conclusions;
9. recommendations;
10. complete appendices.

**Figures:**

- Each arm keeps one color across all twelve charts; the arms before the fix are paler versions of their fixed counterparts' family.
- The two diagrams use the diagram-design default skin, with burnt-tangerine as the single accent on the path the fix adds.
- Captions say how to read the figure, not only what it shows.

**Wording:**

- Every speed is a median of paired ratios.
- Every variance is in thousandths.
- Every cost is in cents unless it says dollars; prose that says "less" or "cheaper" gives the amount without a sign.
- Lower is fairer on every measure.
- Interpretation beyond the recorded fields is labeled as such, for example why the gain shrinks with fleet size.

# Report design contract

This report follows `../fairness-measures-2026-10-20/artifact.md`. The formatting authority is `../WaterFlex_Scheduler_Algorithm_Comparison_Explained.docx` (SHA-256 `5366f3017d6f18c15de295ba5fb9c1a014b6222911040f1c5e044845e57f832d`). Its styles.xml, stylesWithEffects.xml, numbering.xml, theme, fontTable.xml and footer parts are kept byte for byte, and the body is replaced.

**Page and type:**

- one portrait Letter section;
- Calibri;
- black headings;
- pale blue DCE6EF table headers;
- alternating FFFFFF and F4F6F8 rows.

**Tables:**

- Header rows repeat, and no table row splits.
- Tables of twelve or fewer rows stay on one page.
- Appendix and wide tables use 8 or 9 pt cell text; body tables use 10 pt.

**Narrative:** each table is explained by a figure next to it. The report runs in this order:

1. scope and instrument;
2. the mechanism (why the gain grows with the fleet);
3. speed;
4. what the extra moves buy;
5. the service;
6. running the verification;
7. conclusions;
8. recommendations;
9. complete appendices.

**Figures:**

- Each image keeps one color across all seven charts; the harness is amber.
- The two diagrams use the diagram-design default skin, with burnt-tangerine as the single accent on the fairness phase or the paired outcome.
- Captions say how to read the figure, not only what it shows.

**Wording:**

- Every speed is a ratio of medians of one run's proposals, after over before.
- Every variance is the DECIMAL128 value to four places.
- Every cost is in cents.
- Absolute rates are the stack's and are never compared with the harness's.
- Interpretation beyond the recorded fields is labeled as such, for example why the stack's ratios sit below the harness's.
